    Button binds

    To bind, click Bind and press the wheel button you want. To detect a pedal, click Detect, then press that pedal all the way and let go within 3 seconds.

    The car is tuned like a Mercedes-AMG A45 S: 421 hp turbo four with some lag, 8-speed dual-clutch on the paddles with auto rev-matching, and all-wheel drive. Sport+ sends most torque to the front and grips. Drift mode sends most torque to the rear. Switch modes with the Drive mode button. The DCT refuses downshifts that would over-rev. Launch control: hold the brake and floor the throttle at a stop, then release the brake. Sport+ has light traction control; Drift mode turns it off.

    Drifting tips: put it in Drift mode, use 2nd or 3rd, turn in and stab the throttle or tap the e-brake, then catch it with countersteer. The tire walls on the outside of corners are solid.

    Keyboard (for testing): W gas, S brake, AD or ←→ steer, ↑ upshift, ↓ downshift, Space e-brake, M drive mode, R reset.
